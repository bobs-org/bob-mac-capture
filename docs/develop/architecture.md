How the panel is built. Externally observable behavior stays in the [reference](https://bobs-org.github.io/reference/mac-capture/); this page names the boundaries that keep it there.

## Layout

`CaptureCore` imports only Foundation and runs `bob` directly through `Process` with argv arrays and an explicit GUI-safe environment. The app target owns the `NSPanel`, the SwiftUI panel, the editor palette (`CaptureEditorPalette`), the grammar gate (`BlockIDRules`: `bob` is the only authority for the grammar), the agenda store, and the card views. Render fixtures for close, adjust, link-move, created-start, Pomodoro-block, and task-block designs render to PNG when `BOB_MAC_CAPTURE_RENDER_DIR` is set.

## Lanes

Subprocess work stays off the hotkey path, which uses a pre-warmed non-activating `NSPanel`. One shared lane serves the agenda (`capture-pomodoros --tasks`); the app-owned `SnoozeWakeScheduler` calls `snooze wake -q -f json` on a dedicated non-cancelling lane, independent of panel visibility, draft contents, and the Agenda setting. The wake-notice coordinator (`SnoozeNoticeCoordinator`) owns the queue, hold, settle delay, bounded revalidation retry, day purge, and stale-callback suppression behind injected receipt, clock, presence, process, defaults, and presentation/record callbacks; the status-item beacon (`StatusItemController`/`StatusItemGlyph`), silent Notification Center records (`NotificationService`), and Settings toggles consume its shared acknowledgement lifecycle without duplicating it. Bob's advertised RFC 3339 target arms the timer for five seconds after its instant. The scheduler coalesces concurrent triggers into one pending follow-up, refreshes the agenda after every settled attempt (including non-writing outcomes and failures), and never cancels an already-started writer when a timer is retimed or the client is reconfigured. Only writing outcomes settle the schedule: `deferred`/`skipped`/unknown replies keep the due target and its 60-second retry, an unchanged `snoozed` block never overwrites a settled outcome, and day rollover drops the old day's facts and in-flight authority. A confirmed old-Bob command rejection disables this lane for the current client session; Bob remains the sole writer and enforces its host pin. Typing `@@` after an absorbable marker and typing `^` / `:` after a complete ID reuse the rewrite lane. Similar-task lookup has its own cancellable lane: it calls `capture-related --format json -- DRAFT` once after 350 ms without edits. The fire-time gate requires a visible panel with no modal owner (no picker, ID prompt, Pomodoro-name prompt, relation-label prompt, pull-in choice, inbox-route choice, stash picker, or template shelf); a retained-draft show fires the lane immediately instead of waiting the settle. The band state keeps existing rows while a new response is pending. One refresh flies at a time with at most one follow-up; a rewrite applies only when the visible draft still matches the submitted draft, and every show, submit, wake, unlock, day change, and clock change revalidates.

## Caches

The agenda snapshot publishes nothing when byte-identical and is never shown when its date is not today. A related-command rejection is cached per Bob executable identity, so an older Bob hides the band without retrying on later drafts. Route completion strips the `@@` sigil like the server; the link picker filters `bob`'s snapshot locally. Capture targets cache at launch and on panel open and invalidate on filtered vault events (never `.git/`, attachments, or dot-path-only batches). Templates check out in app memory; the last-used filing parent persists after a successful submit, and the last inbox-route destination persists per vault directory.

## Failure boundaries

A malformed `capture-parse` span set is ignored instead of partially applied after UTF-8 range validation. Unknown span kinds, contexts, and groups degrade to neutral text, generic rows, and `note` groups. Additive fields decode with `decodeIfPresent`; a mismatched `schema_version` still rejects. Every `bob` call is bounded by `BobProcessClient.defaultTimeout` with reap and `cancelActiveProcess()` on quit. Guard flags (`--refuse-override`, `--require-pull-in-choice`) retry once without the flag on unexpected-argument rejection and remember the answer per executable.
